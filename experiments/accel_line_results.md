# Accelerating camera on one straight line (SIMULATION ONLY)

Camera stays on the initial line of sight: position 2 t + A sin(w t) along it, w = 2 pi / 12 s, no sideways motion. `surge_15`: A = 15 m (peak acceleration 4.1 m/s^2, same as the weave; min speed -5.9 m/s, so it flies backwards part of each cycle). `surge_4`: A = 4 m (peak 1.1 m/s^2; min speed -0.09 m/s, so it also reverses, barely). Everything else as the weaving case: approach_recede drone, gimbal +/-2 deg wobble, 10 Hz, 40 s, same noise models. Generated by `experiments/accel_line.py`.

## Perfect data

Same setup as `run_noise_free` in tests/test_ekf.py: true size 0.55 m, prior mean 0.35 m (size_prior), no pose noise, perfect detector. Signed range error = estimate - truth (relative error in brackets).

| surge_15 | -27.63 m (-18.9%) | -3.08 m (-3.8%) | -0.10 m (-1.0%) | -0.55 m (-0.7%) | 0.546 m |

## Notes

- Runtime: perfect-data runs 0.2 s, everything (2 x 5 x 200 noisy runs) 19 s on a 16-process pool.
- `surge_4` is not strictly forward: with A = 4, min speed is 2 - 4 (2 pi / 12) = -0.09 m/s, so it reverses very slightly near each slowest point. A < 2 / w = 3.82 m would keep it forward.
- Perfect data: `surge_15` makes range observable (final -0.7%, S 0.546 m) but converges much more slowly than weaving (-18.9% at 10 s, while weaving is about -1% by then). `surge_4` converges only partly in 40 s (final -7.1%, S 0.512 m); straight stays at -37% (no observability).
- Noisy runs: `surge_15` falls between straight and weaving (size_prior RMS 27.6%, wrong_prior bias -18.5%; see Reference). `surge_4` is close to the straight camera (size_prior RMS 40.2%).
- The filter is overconfident on the surge paths: surge_15 size_prior has 2-sigma coverage 50.6% and range ANEES 13.6 (ideal 95% / 1.0).
- `surge_15_bearing_only` has a large bias over t >= 10 s (+56.5%) yet only 0.5% of runs end outside 3 sigma: range is overestimated by roughly +70-80% until the closest approach at 28 s and recovers to a few percent by 40 s (checked from runs/accel_line/surge_15_bearing_only.npz).
- NaN runs (state NaN after init, the known log(Z < 0) issue in experiments/nan_runs.md; numpy prints 'invalid value encountered in log' warnings during the run): surge_15_size_prior: seeds 1007, 1026, 1034, 1130, 1147, 1190, 1199; surge_4_size_prior: seeds 1007, 1026, 1034, 1130, 1147, 1190, 1199. These are the same 7 seeds that go NaN in A_approach_straight_size_prior, first NaN around 3 s, so they come from the early filter, not from the surge motion.
