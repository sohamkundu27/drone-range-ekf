# Accelerating camera along the sideways axis (SIMULATION ONLY)

Camera moves only sideways (perpendicular to the initial line of sight), no forward motion: position 2 t + A sin(w t) along that axis, w = 2 pi / 12 s. `lat_surge_15`: A = 15 m (peak acceleration 4.1 m/s^2, same as the weave; min speed -5.9 m/s, so it reverses part of each cycle). `lat_surge_4`: A = 4 m (peak 1.1 m/s^2; min speed -0.09 m/s, so it also reverses, barely). Same speed and acceleration profiles as `surge_15` / `surge_4` (experiments/accel_line_results.md), which move along the line of sight instead. Everything else as the weaving case: approach_recede drone, gimbal +/-2 deg wobble, 10 Hz, 40 s, same noise models. Generated by `experiments/accel_lateral.py`.

## Notes

- Runtime: perfect-data runs 0.5 s, everything (2 x 5 x 200 noisy runs) 20 s on a 16-process pool.
- Like `surge_4`, `lat_surge_4` is not strictly one-directional: min speed 2 - 4 (2 pi / 12) = -0.09 m/s, so it reverses very slightly near each slowest point. `lat_surge_15` reverses for part of every cycle (min speed -5.9 m/s).
- Comparison pairs: `lat_surge_*` vs `surge_*` differ ONLY in the axis of motion (sideways vs along the initial line of sight), with identical speed and acceleration profiles. The `lateral` camera is the constant-velocity sideways baseline (4 m/s, no acceleration).
- size_prior RMS rel err: lat_surge_15 12.6%, lat_surge_4 24.0%, surge_15 27.6%, surge_4 40.2%; lateral and weaving in the Reference table.
- Perfect data, final range error: lat_surge_15 -0.4%, lat_surge_4 -3.8%, surge_15 -0.7%, surge_4 -7.1%, lateral -42.1%, weaving -0.3%.
- `lat_surge_15` reproduces `weaving` almost exactly (RMS rel err within 0.4 percentage points for every variant except bearing_only): both have the same +/-15 m, 12 s sideways oscillation and differ only in the 2 m/s drift direction (sideways vs forward). The sideways ACCELERATION is what matters, and for the same profile sideways beats along-the-line-of-sight motion at both amplitudes.
- NaN runs (state NaN after init, the known log(Z < 0) issue in experiments/nan_runs.md; numpy prints 'invalid value encountered in log' warnings during the run): lat_surge_15_size_prior: 14 runs, seeds 1007, 1010, 1025, 1026, 1030, 1034, 1035, 1089, 1130, 1147, 1154, 1164, 1189, 1190 (also NaN: 6 in surge_15_size_prior, 13 in A_approach_weaving_size_prior); lat_surge_4_size_prior: 7 runs, seeds 1007, 1026, 1034, 1130, 1147, 1190, 1199 (also NaN: 7 in surge_15_size_prior, 6 in A_approach_weaving_size_prior). Median time of the first NaN: lat_surge_15_size_prior 3.4 s, lat_surge_4_size_prior 3.0 s, i.e. early in the run, while range is still very uncertain; most seeds overlap with the existing NaN runs of the reference cameras.
